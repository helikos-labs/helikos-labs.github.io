Helikos is Mount Helicon — in Greek myth the home of the Muses and the spring of all inspiration, struck from the rock by the hoof of Pegasus.

Helikos Labs builds small, AI-native apps in that spirit. The gods keep the heavens — tools named for the deity whose craft they share — while the games are held below, in the stadium, in their honour. One sharp idea at a time, each kept live in the open.

The first computer was Greek. So is this lab.
