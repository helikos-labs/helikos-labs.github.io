About
The Workshop

Helikos is Mount Helicon — in Greek myth the home of the Muses and the spring of inspiration. Helikos Labs takes that as a brief: a place to build small, AI-native tools quickly, ship them, and keep them live in the open.

Each project is a focused experiment rather than a platform. Some are proofs of concept, some are still in development — all are explorable today on their own helikos.dev subdomain.

The naming follows one rule: every app carries the name of a Greek deity or Muse — wisdom, memory, history, music — matched to what it does.

Ship small
One sharp idea per app, built and released fast.
AI-native
Generative models woven into the core, not bolted on.
Name with intent
A Muse or deity whose domain mirrors the app's purpose.
Stay open
Every app is live on the public web, no gate to peek inside.
