Helikos is Mount Helicon — in Greek myth the home of the Muses and the spring of all inspiration, struck from the rock by the hoof of Pegasus.

Helikos Labs builds small, AI-native apps in that spirit: one sharp idea at a time, each named for the deity whose domain it shares — wisdom, memory, history, music — and each kept live in the open.

The first computer was Greek. So is this lab.
