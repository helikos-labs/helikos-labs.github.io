About Helikos

Helikos is Mount Helicon — in Greek myth, the home of the Muses and the spring of inspiration. Helikos Labs takes that as a brief: a place to build small, sharp, AI-native tools quickly, ship them, and learn in the open.

Every project is a focused experiment rather than a sprawling platform. Some are proofs of concept, some are still in development — all are live and explorable on their own helikos.dev subdomain.
