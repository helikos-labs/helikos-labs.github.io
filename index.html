Foundations laid

Helikos weds Mount Helikon — home of the Muses, where the spring of inspiration was struck from the rock by the hoof of Pegasus — with kosmos, the ordered sky. As above, so below.

Helikos Labs builds small, AI-native apps in that spirit. The deities keep the heavens — tools named for the one whose craft they share — while smaller apps glow below, like towns down the mountain. One sharp idea at a time, each kept live in the open.

The oldest computer charted the heavens. So does this lab.
